ABOUT ASTELLAR

ASTELLAR started with a simple idea: make clothes that look good without needing to shout.

We're building the brand around clean design, solid everyday pieces and details that give each drop its own identity. The first collection keeps things simple with one classic tee in black and white.

We're starting small, learning as we go, and building ASTELLAR one piece at a time.
