ABOUT THE PRODUCT

A clean, low-profile tee with a small, intricate compass emblem over the heart — soft, sturdy, and made to accompany you on everyday routes and weekend detours. The classic crewneck and relaxed silhouette keep movement easy; the subtle chest mark reads like a quiet signature rather than loud branding. Wear it under a flannel on cool mornings, layered with a jacket on city nights, or alone when you want a simple piece that hints at curiosity and quiet direction. The fabric has a smooth, slightly dense hand that holds prints crisply and wears in without losing shape. Ethically sourced cotton and OEKO‑TEX certification mean it sits lightly on your conscience as well as your shoulders.

PRODUCT FEATURES

  • 100% cotton solid colors with medium-weight 180 g/m² fabric for durability and comfortable year-round wear
  • Tubular knit (no side seams) and ribbed, seam-free collar for a clean fit and reduced fabric waste
  • Shoulder twill tape stabilizes the neckline and prevents stretching over time
  • Dual printing: DTF for sleeve/inner labels and DTG for main prints, ensuring sharp, detailed artwork
  • Tear-away label, OEKO‑TEX® STANDARD 100 certified, ethically sourced US cotton; made in Nicaragua

CARE INSTRUCTIONS

  • Non-chlorine: bleach as needed
  • Do not iron
  • Do not dryclean
  • Machine wash: cold (max 30C or 90F)
  • Tumble dry: low heat
